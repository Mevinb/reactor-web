04 / FAQ

Nothing hidden behind the download.

Do images leave my device?

No. ReactorX performs AI processing locally on your computer. This website does not receive your images.

Why don’t you host the AI?

Hosting heavy AI inference needs costly GPU infrastructure and adds privacy tradeoffs. ReactorX is designed to run where your files already live.

Where do outputs go?

By default, outputs are saved to outputs/date_00.png.

Does ReactorX need internet access?

Installation needs internet access to fetch software, dependencies, and applicable models. Once installed, the ReactorX application is designed to run locally.

Can I use ReactorX without an NVIDIA GPU?

Yes, where supported, through CPU fallback. Expect slower processing than a supported NVIDIA GPU setup.
